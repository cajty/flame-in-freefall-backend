Comment[Approach]	Flight Data is from the rack instrumentation during testing.  This is identified by a GMT date number based on day of the calendar year during which the test was run, material used and a unique run number.Ops images were taken within ISS during operations and include astronaut and hardware images.Flight TEM data is images of soot samples collected on thin film TEM grids.  These are in folders separated by material and subfolders identified with a run number corresponding to those used in the flight data and whether the sample has been aged or not.  Each .tif specifies the grid coordinate of the image and magnification.Videos show the resistive heater coil on the far right and the smoke (if visible) trailing to the left.  <br>
